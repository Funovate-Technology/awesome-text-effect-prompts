**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
soft pastel stream graphics a cute streamer or VTuber would actually put on stream or post: starting-soon, be-right-back and stream-ended screens, debut announcements, channel banners, schedule posts and info panels. GRAPHIC LANGUAGE: the words sit in rounded retro computer windows and soft UI panels with tiny title bars, three little dot buttons and a slim info bar beneath the title; on starting and ending screens one large empty rounded window is left open for the streamer's avatar or camera; small stickers are scattered around the edges - hearts, four-point sparkles, stars, puffy clouds and ribbon bows - and small notes or instant-photo frames may sit in the corners. LIGHT: soft even daylight, airy and bright, a gentle bloom on highlights, soft shadows only. COLOUR: a cream white or very pale base with two pastel colours (baby pink and sky blue, lilac and mint, or peach and lavender) plus small touches of a third; lots of breathing space; never dark, never neon-saturated. TEXTURE: a faint grid-paper or polka-dot pattern on the background, lace-scalloped edges on one panel, a light paper grain. TIERS: the sweet tier is white, pink and sky blue with lace, clouds and ribbons, very bright; the Y2K pixel tier uses lilac and baby pink with pixel-stepped window corners, pixel buttons and tiny pixel hearts. LETTERFORM COLOR PRIORITY: if the selected font defines its own letterform colour or material, that takes absolute priority; the pastel palette colours the letterforms only when the font does not specify. ATMOSPHERE: the pastel palette, the windows and the stickers always appear around the words regardless of letterform colour. When no specific material is defined by the font: default lighting - the title sits in a rounded window under soft even daylight with a faint soft shadow, on a cream grid-paper background.
```

## Tips

- Short, cheerful words or brief phrases fit best inside the rounded retro window without crowding the soft UI elements.
- To alter the mood while keeping the style, swap the baby pink and sky blue palette for peach and lavender or mint and lilac.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
