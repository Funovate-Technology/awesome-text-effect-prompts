**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
original anime-style character art people use as a profile picture, a matching couple or best-friend pfp pair, a phone wallpaper, a sticker or a chat-server banner. CHARACTERS: original characters, head-and-shoulders or half-body facing the viewer (chibi stickers two to three heads tall); large expressive eyes with layered irises and two white catchlights; hair in clean clumped locks with a glossy highlight band; small simple nose and mouth; light blush. LINE AND SHADING: clean line art, heavier on the silhouette; flat cel shading with one hard shadow tone, or soft airbrushed anime shading with a thin rim light. BACKGROUND: a pastel sky with cumulus clouds, a sunset rooftop with a chain-link fence, drifting cherry blossom petals or a rainy night street with bokeh lights, softly blurred. COMPOSITION: every face centred and fully inside a circle crop; for a matching pair the wide image splits into two equal halves, one person centred in each, same style, palette and background across both, poses that answer each other (two halves of a heart, one shared sky), nothing important on the centre line. Any name or short word stays small in a corner, away from faces. TIERS: the soft slice-of-life tier is pastel pink, sky blue and cream in daylight or golden sunset; the dark moody tier is black, white and grey with heavy ink shadows, screentone grain and exactly one red accent (eyes, a ribbon or a red thread). LETTERFORM COLOR PRIORITY: if the selected font defines its own letterform colour or material, that takes absolute priority; the tier palette colours the letters only when the font does not specify. ATMOSPHERE: the character, shading, backdrop and tier palette always fill the picture around the words regardless of letterform colour. When no specific material is defined by the font: default lighting - the words sit small over the illustration in the tier's own light with a faint soft shadow.
```

## Tips

- Short names, usernames, or single Japanese-inspired words work best to keep the focus on the anime portrait.
- To vary the mood, swap the daylight pastel theme for a rainy night street with glowing bokeh or a sunset rooftop with a chain-link fence.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
