## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
Bold black ink outline tracing every letterform edge — a clean, hard border between 2 and 6 pixels thick, ensuring every letter reads as a distinct graphic element against any background. Ben-Day halftone dot screen underlying all color areas: regular circular ink dots on white paper substrate, creating the characteristic optically-mixed color of classic four-color printing. Color palette operating at maximum primary contrast: action yellow, impact red, force blue, knockout white — the exact pigments of Silver Age comic printing, saturated to their loudest value. Ink energy in the environment: diagonal speed lines radiating from behind letterforms, faint cosmic crackle energy dots scattered in the shadows, halftone dots diffusing into white at the edges of color areas. The letters exist as graphic symbols pressed onto the page — flat, bold, and a pure statement of graphic force. When no specific material is defined by the font: default environment — action yellow fill on each letterform, thick black outline border, Ben-Day halftone dot pattern overlay at 40% across color areas, white knockout background with speed-line radiating marks.
```

**Scene**

```text
Classic Silver Age comic book action panel — newsprint-warm white background. The text in massive impact letterforms explodes at dead center, filling 72% of frame width. Letters filled with action yellow, hard flat black outline 5px thick. Behind the text: a classic starburst explosion burst shape in racing red with jagged geometric spike points radiating outward to all frame edges. Ben-Day halftone dot grid overlays the red burst — dots sharp, regular, dense. Four-color print register: 1px color offset between yellow fill and black outline, authentic printing misalignment. Two thin horizontal speed lines cross behind the burst at 30% opacity. Pure graphic composition, no photography, zero gradients.
```

## Tips

- Short action verbs, sound effects, and punchy single words best match the explosive panel aesthetic.
- You can easily shift the mood by altering the primary color pair, such as swapping the yellow fill and red burst for force blue and white.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
