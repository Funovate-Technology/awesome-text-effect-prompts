```text
Render the text "{{TEXT}}" exactly as written, character by character. The letters of {{TEXT}} appear as clean, solid vector shapes integrated seamlessly into a modern flat cartoon illustration. Surrounding the text are friendly cartoon characters constructed from minimal geometric forms, featuring round heads, neat dot eyes, simple curved smiles, and hair and clothing designed as solid, untextured shapes. All shapes interact with crisp color-against-color edges, completely free of heavy black outlines or contour lines. The entire composition rests on a clean cream or white background overlaid with soft organic blob shapes and plain flat color fields, playfully dotted with small floating accents such as miniature circles, leaves, sparkles, and hearts. The palette consists of four to six bright, saturated solid colors—specifically coral, sunny yellow, sky blue, mint green, and pink. Lighting is flat and shadowless across all elements, using at most a single subtle darker tone per shape for minimal depth, with no glossy highlights or gradients anywhere. Make {{TEXT}} the only legible text in the image.
```

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
the kind of flat cartoon picture people set as a profile picture, split into matching couple or best-friend PFPs, print as stickers or post as a simple illustration. GRAPHIC LANGUAGE: modern flat vector character illustration - friendly people and pets built from simple geometric shapes: round heads, dot eyes, a small curved smile, hair, hands and clothes as single solid shapes. Edges are colour against colour, with no heavy contour lines. Each character sits on one plain colour field or a soft organic blob, with a few small shapes around it: circles, leaves, sparkles, hearts. A single avatar keeps head and shoulders large and centred; a matching pair is two characters side by side, each centred in its own half on its own colour field, echoing each other in pose and palette so each half works alone as a profile picture. LIGHT: flat even fills with one soft darker shadow tone per shape, no gradients or gloss. COLOUR: four to six solid colours per picture. TEXTURE: crisp vector edges, at most faint background grain. TIERS: the playful bright tier uses saturated coral, sunny yellow, sky blue, mint and pink on cream or white (cartoon avatars, stickers, cheerful posts); the muted earthy tier uses terracotta, mustard, sage, dusty blue and warm beige with more empty space (calm couple PFPs, editorial illustrations). Characters are original, never famous cartoon or anime characters, never with big shiny anime eyes. LETTERFORM COLOR PRIORITY: if the selected font defines its own letterform colour or material, that takes absolute priority; the tier palette colours the letters only when the font does not specify. ATMOSPHERE: the flat cartoon characters, plain colour fields, blob shapes and small scattered shapes in the palette always appear around the words regardless of letterform colour. When no specific material is defined by the font: default lighting - the words sit on a plain flat colour field under even shadowless light next to the flat cartoon characters.
```

## Tips

- Short, cheerful words like first names, greetings, or playful nicknames best complement the cute sticker-style character arrangement.
- To achieve a more mature or calm editorial feel, replace the bright coral and mint palette with earthy tones like terracotta, sage, mustard, and warm beige.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
