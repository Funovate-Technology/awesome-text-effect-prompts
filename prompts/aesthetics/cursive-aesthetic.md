## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
styled as a soft, dreamy coquette aesthetic. SURFACE / INK: the lettering has a delicate, pretty finish — soft matte or a whisper of pearly shimmer, with a faint dusting of fine sparkle catching the light, sweet and dainty rather than bold. GROUND: a soft, dreamy backdrop — blush pink, cream, pastel lilac or a gentle gradient — with airy negative space, tiny out-of-focus sparkles, pearls, ribbons or a soft bokeh glow. LIGHT: soft, diffused and glowy — a gentle dreamy light with no harsh shadows, the mood hazy and romantic. ATMOSPHERE: the sweet, girly charm of a soft-girl / coquette aesthetic — social media bios, journal pages, dreamy mood boards, delicate and playful. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own treatment, that font's letterform shapes and flourishes take priority and own the letters; this style governs only the finish, ground and light, never the letter shapes. When no specific material is defined by the font: a soft delicate lettering finish with a faint pearly shimmer and a light dusting of fine sparkle, on the dreamy pastel ground. COLOR: if the font declares its own color keep it; otherwise the lettering reads as a soft romantic tone — a delicate blush pink, soft rose or a gentle charcoal — on the pastel ground.
```

**Scene**

```text
The word sits large and centered with airy negative space on a soft, dreamy backdrop — blush pink, cream or pastel lilac with a gentle gradient, tiny out-of-focus sparkles, pearls or a soft bokeh glow. Soft, diffused dreamy light, no harsh shadows. Sweet, girly soft-girl coquette mood.
```

## Tips

- Short names, affectionate pet names, or gentle romantic words naturally complement the delicate, dreamy look.
- Adjust the background gradient toward pure cream or soft lavender to subtly alter the warmth of the scene.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
