## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
Aggressive forward-lean italic attitude: every letterform tilts at an exaggerated angle, implying high-velocity motion even in stillness. Speed lines — fine horizontal streaks — trail behind strokes, fading from sharp near the letterform edge to dissolving into the background, mimicking the visual physics of a body moving faster than the eye can track. High-energy visual weight: thick strokes command visual gravity, negative space is tight and compressed, every letterform reads like it is under aerodynamic load. Racing livery surface environment: hard studio light source directly overhead or slightly to the front, casting a sharp specular axis along the top plane of each stroke. Racing color palette activated: fierce reds, championship gold, carbon-black shadows, pure white speed streaks. When no specific material is defined by the font: default lighting environment — single hard overhead beam, deep shadow falling behind each letterform in the direction of motion, pure black track-surface background, faint motion-haze atmospheric scatter in the air around the letters.
```

```text
Race track asphalt fills the lower quarter of the frame — dark grey tarmac with faint painted white line. Letters rendered in extreme condensed italic, filling 75% of frame width at center-left, tilted forward at maximum racing lean. Hard overhead spotlight beam, cold white. Crisp specular axis along each stroke top, deep carbon shadow beneath. Background: full darkness of night circuit, faint lens flare from a distant track light at upper right. Fine horizontal speed lines trail behind each letterform, dissolving rightward. Cinematic pit lane atmosphere. Wide 16:9.
```

## Tips

- Short, punchy action words or velocity-themed terms pair most naturally with the extreme forward tilt and horizontal speed streaks.
- Swap the red and gold livery colors for electric blues or high-visibility yellows to evoke different competitive team aesthetics.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
