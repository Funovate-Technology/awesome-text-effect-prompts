## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
ancient Norse warrior aesthetic — stark angular geometry, battle-forged weight, the material permanence of things built to outlast kingdoms. Directional forge-light or cold torch-light that sculpts every letterform face into high relief. Color palette: iron-grey, aged bronze, cold granite, dark amber — no soft pastels, no unmotivated gradients. Every letter reads as an object with real physical mass.

When no specific material is defined by the font: default to battle-worn dark iron — cold charcoal-grey letter body with visible forge-hammer surface texture, narrow cold specular highlight on the upper stroke edge, deep iron shadow on the underside, hairline stress-fracture lines along diagonal strokes. The Norse blacksmith's iron: heavy, angular, built for war.
```

**Scene**

```text
Dramatic overcast sky, cold blue-gray atmosphere, torch fire lighting casting warm flickering highlights, ancient Scandinavian landscape.
```

## Tips

- Short, forceful words or titles emphasizing strength, myth, or warfare harmonize best with this heavy iron aesthetic.
- Adjust the lighting balance between the cold blue ambient sky and the warm amber torch glow to shift the mood from bleak winter to a fiery forge.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
