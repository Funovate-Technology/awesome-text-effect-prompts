## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
Warm enchanted light environment — magical golden-amber luminosity fills the space from all sides, as if the air itself is enchanted: no single hard light source, but a diffuse warm glow that wraps all surfaces with candlelit warmth. Secondary light: cool silver-white moonlight touches from directly above, creating subtle top-catch highlights. Background palette: deep midnight blue to forest shadow black — rich darkness that makes warm magic visible. Atmospheric particles: fine light-dust drifts freely in the illuminated space — the ambient radiance of an enchanted world, not stuck-on decorative sparkles. Color tonality: warm gold and amber as dominant warmth, rose-pink as secondary accent, silver-white as moonlit crown catch.

When no specific material is defined by the font: default fairy tale light field — soft warm-amber ambient illumination wrapping all surfaces, gentle silver moonlight from above, deep midnight-blue to forest-black background, fine floating light-dust in air.
```

**Scene**

```text
Enchanted world at night receding into soft bokeh depth behind the text — distant castle silhouette against moonlit sky, or ancient forest canopy with silver starlight filtering through, or candlelit storybook interior dissolving in warm shadow. Illumination: warm golden ambient light rising from within the scene, as if from an enchanted source below and around — not direct sunlight, not neon. Floating micro-particles: soft firefly traces far behind, gossamer sparkle in deep background layers, light-haze giving the air magical luminosity. Composition: text occupies the center third of the frame, bokeh depth pushing the enchanted world into dreamlike softness behind.
```

## Tips

- Words evocative of folklore, magic, or storybook titles pair naturally with this warm nocturnal aesthetic.
- Adjust the balance between the cool silver-white starlight and the warm golden-amber glow to dial the mood between an icy enchanted night and a cozy twilight tale.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
