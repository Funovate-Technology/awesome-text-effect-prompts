## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
a dark, dramatic gothic piece, ominous and atmospheric. SURFACE / STYLE: heavy gothic lettering forged from cold dark material — black iron with hammer marks and rust, or deep-carved weathered stone, or wet black surfaces — with cobweb strands stretching across the gaps and beads of cold moisture. SCENE ATMOSPHERE: deep shadow pierced by dramatic crimson backlighting that burns glowing red edges around the letters, faint drifting smoke or mist, the air of a dungeon, crypt or gothic cathedral. PALETTE: near-black and charcoal grounds with deep blood-crimson light and cold steel highlights — dark and saturated, never bright. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own treatment (iron blackletter, dripping blood, stained glass, flame, engraved tombstone, metal-band logo, ornate tattoo), that font treatment takes absolute priority and owns the letters; this style governs the dark material, the crimson backlight, the cobweb-and-smoke atmosphere and the crypt scene. FALLBACK MATERIAL: if the font carries no material of its own, render the letters as cold dark iron or weathered stone with a crimson rim-light burning behind them, faint cobweb and cold moisture, against a near-black ground, so the dark gothic dread always reads.
```

**Scene**

```text
The text stands bold and dominant in deep shadow, dramatic crimson backlighting burning glowing red edges around it, faint cobweb strands and drifting smoke in the gaps, set in a cold gothic crypt, dungeon or cathedral of damp dark stone. Ominous, atmospheric, dread-soaked dark-gothic mood.
```

## Tips

- Short, imposing words or dark, archaic terms work best with this heavy, ominous aesthetic.
- Try shifting the crimson backlighting to a pale spectral blue or sickly emerald green to change the supernatural tone of the crypt.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
