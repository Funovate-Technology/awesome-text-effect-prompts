```text
an opulent 1920s Art Deco piece — geometric, symmetrical and luxurious. FALLBACK MATERIAL: if the font carries no material of its own, render the letters as polished gold-and-chrome Art Deco metal with fine pinline engraving and beveled facets on a black lacquer ground, framed by a symmetrical sunburst, so the 1920s deco luxury always reads.
```

## Tips

- Short, capitalized words or single impactful terms best complement the rigid symmetry and architectural framework of the design.
- Introduce deep emerald or rich oxblood as the primary lacquer ground to shift the mood while maintaining high-contrast metallic luxury.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
