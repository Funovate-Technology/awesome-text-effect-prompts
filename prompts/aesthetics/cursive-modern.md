## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
grounded in bold, contemporary brush-lettering ink. SURFACE / INK: rich, confident ink laid down with a loaded brush — mostly matte with a faint fresh-ink sheen, a subtle brushed texture in the strokes and a touch of dry-brush grain where they lift, energetic and hand-made. GROUND: a clean, modern surface — a bright studio backdrop, a soft neutral, a bold flat colour block, or a contemporary branding/packaging context — with generous space so the lettering pops. LIGHT: clean, even, contemporary studio light — punchy and bright, minimal drama. ATMOSPHERE: current, confident and commercial — the look of modern brand lettering, apparel graphics, packaging and social content. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own treatment, that font's letterform shapes take priority and own the letters; this style governs only the ink surface, ground and light, never the letter shapes. When no specific material is defined by the font: rich confident brush ink, mostly matte with a subtle brushed texture, on the clean modern ground. COLOR: if the font declares its own color keep it; otherwise the lettering reads as a bold contemporary tone — deep black or a single confident colour — on the clean ground.
```

**Scene**

```text
The word sits large and centered with generous space on a clean, modern surface — a bright studio backdrop, a soft neutral or a bold flat colour block. Clean, even, punchy contemporary studio light makes the bold brush lettering pop. Current, confident, commercial modern-branding feel.
```

## Tips

- Short, punchy words or action verbs suit this flowing, energetic brush style best.
- Try changing the background to a bold, flat color block instead of a neutral backdrop to create a high-contrast graphic look.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
