## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
placed in a soft, refined calligraphy light field — a gentle directional light rakes across the surface from an upper angle, grazing the strokes so their natural ink body and subtle sheen read with depth, while a soft fill keeps shadows intimate rather than harsh. GROUND: a clean elegant art surface — fine hot-press cotton paper, smooth cream card, a soft neutral or a quiet dark editorial ground — with generous negative space so the lettering breathes. ATMOSPHERE: the timeless, romantic feel of masterful pen calligraphy — poised and versatile, at home on a name, a quote, a bio or a card. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own treatment, that font's letterform shapes and built-in flourishes take priority and own the letters; this style governs only the surrounding light, ground and finish, never the letter shapes. When no specific material is defined by the font: rich flowing ink with a soft natural sheen and a fine wet-edge glisten where strokes catch the light, on the clean paper ground. COLOR: if the font declares its own color keep it; otherwise the ink reads as a deep elegant tone — classic near-black or a soft romantic ink hue — on the light ground.
```

**Scene**

```text
The word sits large and centered with generous negative space on a clean elegant art surface — fine cream cotton paper, a smooth ivory card or a quiet dark editorial ground. A gentle directional light rakes across from an upper angle, grazing the strokes so the flowing ink body and its soft sheen read with depth. Timeless, romantic pen-calligraphy feel — at home on a name, a quote or a card.
```

## Tips

- Short names, romantic words, or single celebratory sentiments suit the open composition and generous negative space best.
- Try changing the cream paper ground to a quiet dark charcoal surface or specifying a deep walnut or jewel-toned ink to adjust the mood.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
