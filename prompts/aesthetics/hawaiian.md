## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
Tropical tiki bar atmosphere — deep aged mahogany surfaces with natural bamboo grain running horizontally across the letterforms, shallow relief carved texture suggesting hand-carved island woodwork. Warm overhead sunlight blazing from above creates bright golden highlights on raised bamboo ridges and deep amber shadow in carved recesses. Coral orange rim light from the side edge catches every groove. The material environment is saturated island warmth: deep tropical brown, burnished coral gold, leaf shadow green in the background. Hibiscus blossoms and broad palm fronds surround the composition as environmental elements — not decorating the letters but present as living tropical backdrop. Forbidden: cold steel or chrome surfaces, urban concrete backgrounds, snow or ice textures, any muted or desaturated palette.
```

**Scene**

```text
Deep tropical nightfall tiki bar setting — weathered dark wood plank walls with coconut shell and tiki torch sconces casting warm amber pools of light. Text centered with generous breathing room, set against a dark bamboo-paneled wall draped with fresh plumeria flower garlands. Tiki torches frame the left and right edges, their flame glow warming the scene edges with flickering orange. Ground level scattered with tropical flowers in soft focus. Atmosphere: golden-hour warmth, lush, celebratory.
```

## Tips

- Short island-themed phrases, vacation greetings, or cocktail names harmonize best with this bold, hand-carved woodwork style.
- To adjust the mood, you can transition the scene from nightfall torchlight to daytime by replacing the torch flames with bright, dappled sunbeams filtering through palm fronds.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
