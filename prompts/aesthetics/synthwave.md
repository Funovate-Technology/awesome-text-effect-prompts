## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
a vivid 1980s synthwave / retrowave world, lit entirely by an impossible neon sunset. LIGHT: the key light is the sunset itself — a horizontal band of cool cyan-teal high, hot magenta-pink through the middle, warm orange-red low — joined by magenta and cyan neon sources that wrap every form in a double-color rim glow with soft bloom. SURFACE: glossy and reflective — metal and glass mirror the gradient sky (cyan caught up high, magenta and orange caught low) with one bright horizontal specular streak like a chrome horizon line; blacks stay rich, saturation pushed beyond reality. TEXTURE: a faint analog layer over everything — subtle VHS grain, fine horizontal scanlines, gentle chromatic-aberration fringing at high-contrast edges. PALETTE: hot magenta, electric cyan, sunset orange, deep violet-to-indigo night. FALLBACK MATERIAL: if the chosen font carries no material of its own, render the letters as glossy chrome reflecting the sunset gradient — cyan top, magenta middle, orange base — edged with a magenta-and-cyan neon glow, so the synthwave identity always reads.
```

**Scene**

```text
A deep synthwave night receding to a glowing horizon: a neon perspective grid floor runs to a vanishing point where a striped retro sun sits low, a magenta-to-violet gradient sky rises above with scattered stars, distant wireframe mountains or palm-tree silhouettes at the edges. The text sits large and dominant in the upper-center, owning the frame, the horizon line passing behind it. Cinematic 80s retrowave atmosphere — glossy, saturated, nostalgic for a 1980s that never was.
```

## Tips

- Short, punchy words in all-caps suit this aesthetic best, giving the chrome reflections and glowing outlines maximum surface area.
- To vary the atmosphere, emphasize either the wireframe mountain landscape for a digital look or silhouetted palm trees for a coastal evening feel.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
