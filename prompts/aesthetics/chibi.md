## Standalone prompt

Replace `{{TEXT}}` with your words. Written for general image models; tested on `gemini-2.5-flash-image`.

```text
Render the text "{{TEXT}}" exactly as written, character by character. The lettering sits neatly in open space beside or below an invented super-deformed cute chibi character. The character stands two to three heads tall with an oversized head, tiny rounded hands and feet, rosy blush cheeks, a small happy mouth, and large shiny eyes with bright double white highlights, posed cheerfully waving or holding a heart. Thick, soft line art of uniform weight in dark brown outlines both the character and the letters, filled with flat and two-tone cel shading. The letters are styled in harmonious pastel tones matching the palette. The overall composition features a pastel base of baby pink, lavender, mint, and butter yellow, complemented by one deeper tone for clothing or hair. Surrounding the text and character are floating miniature hearts, sparkles, and puffy little clouds. Soft, even lighting illuminates the piece with gentle cel shadows under the chin and hair. A wide, smooth white die-cut sticker border hugs the outer contour against a plain pale pastel background.
```

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
the kind of chibi art people set as their profile picture, swap with a partner or best friend as matching PFPs, print as stickers, use as streaming or chat emotes or keep as a wallpaper. GRAPHIC LANGUAGE: invented super-deformed cute characters two to three heads tall, the head a third to half of the height, tiny rounded hands and feet, big shiny eyes with two white highlights, a small mouth and rosy blush cheeks, in one simple pose (waving, hugging a plushie, holding a heart). A single piece shows one character with its face in the middle; a matching pair gives each character its own half with the face in the middle of that half, drawn the same way and reaching toward or mirroring each other so the halves read as a set. Small hearts, sparkles and tiny clouds float around them; any words sit small in open space beside or below. LIGHT: soft even light, one gentle cel shadow under the chin and hair, tiny white highlights on eyes and hair. COLOUR: a pastel base (baby pink, mint, lavender, butter yellow or sky blue), pink cheeks, one deeper tone for hair or clothes. TEXTURE: thick soft line art of even weight in dark brown, flat or two-tone cel fills. TIERS: the sticker tier puts each character on a plain pale background with a wide white die-cut border hugging the silhouette (avatars, stickers, emotes); the scene tier sets them in a small cozy pastel setting - a bedroom corner, a cafe, a starry sky (wallpapers, matching PFPs, avatars). LETTERFORM COLOR PRIORITY: if the selected font defines its own letterform colour or material, that takes absolute priority; the pastel palette colours the letters only when the font does not specify. ATMOSPHERE: the chibi characters, the pastel palette, the hearts and sparkles and the soft even light always appear around the words regardless of letterform colour. When no specific material is defined by the font: default lighting - the words sit on a pale pastel background under soft even light beside the chibi characters.
```

## Tips

- Short names, playful nicknames, or single cheerful words fit best alongside the compact chibi figure without crowding the sticker silhouette.
- Swap the white die-cut sticker border for a cozy pastel cafe or starry night background to turn the design into a miniature scene.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
