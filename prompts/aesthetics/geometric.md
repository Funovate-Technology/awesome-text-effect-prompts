**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
Bauhaus geometric environment: hard overhead directional light — single cold-white source, zero ambient scatter, ruler-sharp geometric shadow edges below all forms. Background plane is architecturally flat: pure black void, deep matte concrete, or stark white plaster. No depth haze, no organic texture, no atmospheric bokeh. Scene vocabulary: Bauhaus workshop geometry, De Stijl primary-color planes, Constructivist poster surface, Swiss grid precision.

Primary color atmosphere (always active): flat geometric primary-color planes — signal red, cobalt blue, cadmium yellow — appear as background architectural blocks or compositional color bars alongside the letterforms. Zero gradient, zero sheen.

When no specific material is defined by the font: letterforms rendered with Bauhaus primary color flat-paint treatment — each letter one vivid matte hue cycling across the primary palette: signal red, cobalt blue, cadmium yellow, flat black. Hard 45-degree isometric extrusion with pure-black side wall, depth 25% of letter height. Flat matte acrylic surface, zero sheen. Sharp machined edge between color face and extrusion wall.
```

**Scene**

```text
Text centered, filling 78% of frame width. Background: pure flat black — absolute architectural void, no visible floor, no horizon. Head-on camera angle, zero perspective tilt. 4:3 format.
```

## Tips

- Short words, single terms, or all-caps acronyms work best with this style's cycling primary color pattern.
- Switch the flat black background to stark white plaster or deep matte concrete to dramatically alter the contrast without losing the strict geometric feel.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
