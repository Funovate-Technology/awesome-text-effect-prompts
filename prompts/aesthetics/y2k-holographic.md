## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
Y2K holographic gradient letters — vivid iridescent surface shifting from hot pink to electric purple to cyan blue. Holographic foil shimmer quality. Small starburst sparkle accents around letter edges. Early 2000s party poster aesthetic. Saturated millennium color palette. Premium holographic foil quality. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own treatment, that font's letterform shapes take priority and own the letters; this style governs the holographic-foil shimmer, the iridescent gradient and the sparkle accents. FALLBACK MATERIAL: if the font carries no material of its own, render the letters as vivid iridescent holographic-foil letters shifting hot pink to electric purple to cyan blue with small starburst sparkles. COLOR PRIORITY: keep the font's own colors if specified; otherwise the saturated millennium pink-purple-cyan iridescence.
```

**Scene**

```text
Dark holographic backdrop with scattered light prism effects, faint rainbow light streaks fanning outward from the edges of the frame.
```

## Tips

- Short, punchy words, acronyms, or club-themed phrases fit the high-energy millennium party aesthetic especially well.
- Adjust the background darkness or brightness of the rainbow light streaks to vary between a subtle glow and an intense concert-poster mood.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
