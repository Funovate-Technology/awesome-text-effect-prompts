## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
a timeless vintage piece carrying the physical evidence of an older making — warm, aged and classic. SURFACE / STYLE: the lettering looks produced by a heritage craft and softened by time — a letterpress impression bitten into cotton paper with ink slightly broken up, weathered paint on aged metal or wood, a worn rubber-stamp or postal imprint, or warm gold leaf — carrying patina, foxing, grain and gentle wear. SCENE ATMOSPHERE: a warm nostalgic setting — aged cream or kraft paper, dark wood, soft amber light, faint dust and a faded heritage feel. PALETTE: warm aged tones — cream, kraft, sepia, faded red, forest green, navy, tan and antique gold — muted and time-softened, never neon-bright. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own treatment (western slab, art deco, art nouveau, letterpress, postal or rubber stamp, gold leaf, typewriter, hand-lettered script, grunge chalk), that font treatment takes absolute priority and owns the letters; this style supplies the warm aged tone, the time-worn surface and the nostalgic heritage scene. FALLBACK MATERIAL: if the font carries no material of its own, render the letters as a classic serif printed in warm faded ink on aged cream paper with a soft letterpress impression and gentle wear, so the timeless vintage feel always reads.
```

**Scene**

```text
The text sits bold and classic, printed or worn onto an aged surface — cream or kraft paper, weathered wood, or old painted metal — under warm amber light, with soft dust, foxing and the gentle patina of age. Timeless, nostalgic heritage mood.
```

## Tips

- Short titles, classic trade occupations, or single heritage words pair naturally with the letterpress aesthetic.
- Describe the background as weathered dark wood or chipped enamel metal instead of cream paper to transform the look into an old mercantile sign.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
