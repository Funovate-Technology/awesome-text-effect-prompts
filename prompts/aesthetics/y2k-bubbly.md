## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
Y2K bubbly inflated letters — extremely round pillow-soft letter forms completely inflated like over-pressurized balloons. Pastel pink-to-lavender gradient across the glossy candy surface. Strong white specular highlight on the top of each inflated letter. Extremely smooth and soft surface. Sweet 2000s Y2K party aesthetic. Maximum bubbly inflation quality. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own treatment, that font's letterform shapes take priority and own the letters; this style governs the over-inflated pillow form, the glossy candy surface and the speculars. FALLBACK MATERIAL: if the font carries no material of its own, render the letters as extremely round over-inflated pillow letters with an ultra-smooth glossy candy surface and a strong white top specular. COLOR PRIORITY: keep the font's own colors if specified; otherwise a pastel pink-to-lavender gradient.
```

**Scene**

```text
Pastel dreamy backdrop in soft cotton candy pink fading to lavender, scattered tiny star confetti, warm diffused soft-box lighting.
```

## Tips

- Short, playful words, pet names, or abbreviations look best with this intensely puffed, bubbly treatment.
- You can easily shift the palette by swapping the pink-to-lavender gradient for pastel mint, peach, or baby blue while retaining the glossy top specular highlights.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
