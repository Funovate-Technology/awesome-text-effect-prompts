## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
24-karat gold metallic surface — three-tone spectral gradient: deep amber-brown in shadow recesses, rich warm gold on lit faces, razor-thin champagne-white specular lines where direct light strikes. Single hard key light from upper-left at 45°, casting crisp directional shadows to lower-right. Background: near-black with faint warm amber ambient glow. No colored tints — pure dark void that forces the gold to own all luminosity in the frame. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own treatment, that font's letterform shapes take priority and own the letters; this style governs the 24-karat gold metal, the hard directional key light and the dark void. FALLBACK MATERIAL: if the font carries no material of its own, render the letters as bold 3D-extruded 24-karat gold with deep amber shadow recesses, rich warm gold faces and razor champagne-white speculars. COLOR PRIORITY: keep the font's own colors if specified; otherwise the warm amber-to-champagne gold range on a near-black ground.
```

**Scene**

```text
Letters centered in frame against a near-black background, key light from upper-left casting sharp diagonal shadows. Subtle warm gold ambient light pools on the ground plane beneath. Award ceremony energy — timeless, heavyweight, prestige.
```

## Tips

- Short, punchy words or single ceremonial titles suit this heavyweight 3D style best.
- Adjust the key light angle from upper-left to directly overhead if you want stronger downward shadow drops and heightened top-edge highlights.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
