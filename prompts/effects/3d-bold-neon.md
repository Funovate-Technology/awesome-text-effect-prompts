## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
Neon-lit surfaces with electric edge lighting in synthwave colors — hot magenta, electric cyan, sunset orange. Deep cast shadows with colored neon light bleeding onto surrounding surfaces. 80s retrowave energy. Premium CGI quality. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own treatment, that font's letterform shapes take priority and own the letters; this style governs the electric neon edge lighting, the colored light bleed and the 3D depth. FALLBACK MATERIAL: if the font carries no material of its own, render the letters as bold 3D letters with electric neon edge lighting and colored neon light bleeding onto the surrounding surfaces. COLOR PRIORITY: keep the font's own colors if specified; otherwise hot magenta, electric cyan and sunset orange synthwave.
```

**Scene**

```text
Dark city rooftop at dusk, neon signs reflected in puddles on wet concrete below, electric grid lines faintly visible in the hazy sky behind.
```

## Tips

- Short, punchy words or vintage arcade-inspired titles pair best with the bold 3D structure and intense neon glow.
- Adjust the dominant color by requesting either cyan or hot magenta to take lead over the scene's ambient lighting and puddle reflections.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
