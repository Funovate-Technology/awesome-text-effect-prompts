Warm light halo and bloom around each letter, slight buzzing energy feel. COLOR PRIORITY: keep the font's own colors if specified; otherwise soft pink and amber.

**Scene**

```text
Nighttime exterior wall with visible brick texture, the sign's warm light glowing against the dark, a faint rain shimmer on the bricks and a soft halo bloom in the night air. Nostalgic Americana roadside aesthetic.
```

## Tips

- Short, punchy words or single expressions work best to keep the glowing glass bends clear and readable.
- Switch the neon tube colors from soft pink and amber to electric turquoise or deep cherry red to instantly shift the sign's mood.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
