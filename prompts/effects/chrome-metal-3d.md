COLOR PRIORITY: keep the font's own colors if specified; otherwise the cool chrome blue-grey with electric-blue rim.

## Tips

- Short, capitalized words work best to showcase the broad bevels and dramatic edge highlights.
- Adjust the color of the rim lighting to cyan, amber, or violet to subtly alter the temperature of the scene while retaining the chrome finish.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
