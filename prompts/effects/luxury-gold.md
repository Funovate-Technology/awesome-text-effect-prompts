## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
Liquid 24-karat gold molten surface — microscopic foil crinkle texture across all faces, deep amber pooling in recesses, rich warm gold across main surfaces, razor-bright champagne-white specular peaks where direct light strikes. Subtle embossed relief depth — each letterform has physical mass and weight, like cast or stamped precious metal with visible surface thickness. Single dramatic spotlight from upper-left, angled 45°, creates crisp directional shadow and intense highlight contrast. Background: pure deep black — zero ambient fill, forcing all luminosity to belong to the gold.
```

**Scene**

```text
Letters centered in frame, filling 70-80% of canvas width. Pure deep black background — high contrast to maximise gold luminosity. Optional: subtle warm amber light pool on the ground plane beneath letters.
```

## Tips

- Short single words or punchy acronyms work best to maximize letter thickness and showcase the fine crinkled foil details.
- Remove the mention of the amber ground pool to make the gleaming gold letters appear as though they are floating weightlessly in deep space.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
