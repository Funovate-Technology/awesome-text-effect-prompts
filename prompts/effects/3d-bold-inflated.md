## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
rendered as hyper-glossy 3D inflated forms — smooth candy lacquer surface with a strong circular specular highlight at each convex peak. Vivid Y2K candy color palette: each letter independently colored in saturated bubblegum pink, mint green, electric lavender, or sky blue. Soft ambient occlusion in the deep crevices between letters. Gentle soft drop shadow below, slightly blurred. Playful volumetric depth from studio overhead key light. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own treatment, that font's letterform shapes take priority and own the letters; this style governs the glossy inflated 3D form, the candy lacquer surface and the overhead key light. FALLBACK MATERIAL: if the font carries no material of its own, render the letters as hyper-glossy inflated 3D letters with a strong circular specular highlight at each convex peak and candy-lacquer surface. COLOR PRIORITY: keep the font's own colors if specified; otherwise give each letter a vivid Y2K candy hue (bubblegum pink, mint green, electric lavender, sky blue).
```

**Scene**

```text
Vivid Y2K candy colors, hyper-glossy inflated surface with strong circular specular highlights at each convex peak. Pastel gradient or dark candy-store studio background. Ambient occlusion in letter crevices, soft drop shadow below. Joyful party energy, maximum visual impact.
```

## Tips

- Short, punchy words maximize the puffy, inflated look of the letters.
- Switching the pastel background to a dark candy-store studio backdrop will make the glossy reflections and vibrant candy colors pop with higher contrast.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
