## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
a playful inflated bubble-letter piece — glossy, soft and candy-bright. SURFACE / STYLE: the lettering is rendered as puffy inflated bubble forms with a soft translucent jelly-like material, a glossy reflective surface with bright highlight spots and gentle light refraction, rounded and pillowy. SCENE ATMOSPHERE: a light cheerful setting — a soft gradient backdrop, floating bubbles and sparkle, an airy bubblegum mood. PALETTE: candy colors — bubblegum pink, lavender, sky blue, mint and lemon — bright, glossy and sweet, never dark or matte. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own treatment (bubble rounded, inflated bubble, bubble outline, bouncy display, candy drip), that font's letterform takes priority and owns the letters; this style governs the glossy inflated jelly material, the candy gradient and the airy bubble scene. FALLBACK MATERIAL: if the font carries no material of its own, render the letters as puffy inflated glossy bubbles in a candy pink-purple-blue gradient with bright highlight spots and soft refraction, so the bubbly fun always reads.
```

**Scene**

```text
The text sits puffy and glossy as inflated candy bubbles with bright highlight spots and soft light refraction, set against a light pastel gradient with floating bubbles and sparkle. Playful, sweet, airy bubblegum mood.
```

## Tips

- Short, punchy words and playful exclamations suit the rounded, buoyant forms best.
- You can narrow the color palette to specific pairings, such as mint and sky blue or pink and lemon, to evoke specific candy flavors.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
