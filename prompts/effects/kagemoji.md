## Standalone prompt

Replace `{{TEXT}}` with your words. Written for general image models; tested on `gemini-2.5-flash-image`.

```text
Render the text "{{TEXT}}" exactly as written, character by character. The letters of "{{TEXT}}" are rendered in a flat, solid cream-white. Every single letter casts a bold, exaggerated long shadow extending toward the lower-right at a precise 45-degree angle, stretching 2.5 to 3 times the height of the letters across the background. This shadow is rendered in a uniform, flat, solid deep brown-black tone with sharp, crisp, straight boundaries, entirely devoid of gradients, blur, glows, bevels, or vanishing points, maintaining a purely flat graphic silhouette rather than a three-dimensional block. The scene is illuminated by an implied single light source from the upper-left. The overall image presents a clean vintage print aesthetic in a warm, muted retro palette, positioned against a completely flat, solid-color background such as mustard yellow, burnt orange, faded teal, or olive green, without any photographic textures or gradients. The text is centered, occupying approximately 70% of the frame width with generous, even margins on all sides, ensuring {{TEXT}} is the sole visual element.
```

## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
LONG SHADOW (mandatory, every letter): each letter casts a second, darker copy of its shape, dragged toward the lower-right at 45 degrees, 2.5-3x the letter height -- large, unmistakable, covering much of the background. NOT a plain letter with no shadow. NOT a small drop shadow. The shadow is one flat solid color with a crisp straight edge: no gradient, no blur, no glow, no bevel, no vanishing point -- flat, not a 3D block.

COLOR: font's own color takes priority for the letter; the shadow uses a darker tone of it. No font color -> cream-white letter, deep brown-black shadow.

ATMOSPHERE: a flat-graphic register -- no photographic texture, no gradient sky -- in a warm muted retro palette (mustard, burnt orange, faded teal, olive), one consistent light source from the upper-left, like a printed vintage text-effect template rather than a photograph.
```

**Scene**

```text
Centered on a flat solid-color background, filling about 70% of the frame width, generous even margin on all sides, no other elements in frame.
```

## Tips

- Short, punchy words work best, giving the long 45-degree shadow projections ample space to extend across the background without overlapping cluttered edges.
- You can change the background color to specific retro shades like olive green, mustard, or burnt orange to alter the mood while preserving the crisp graphic style.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
