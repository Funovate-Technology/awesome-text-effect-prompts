Replace `{{TEXT}}` with your words. Written for general image models; tested on `gemini-2.5-flash-image`.

```text
Render the text "{{TEXT}}" exactly as written, character by character. The letters are crafted from cold brushed titanium featuring precision-machined edges, micro-etched technical detail lines, and an aerospace-grade matte finish with subtle anisotropic grain. Cryogenic blue-white edge illumination radiates coldly from the letter seams, producing sharp, laser-cut thresholds against the surrounding dark. Lighting arrives from a single distant star entering from the upper left, casting razor-edge directional shadows across the letterforms, complemented by a lone horizontal cyan-white anamorphic streak. The letters float in a hard vacuum environment with zero ambient scatter and maximum contrast. Behind {{TEXT}}, a colossal megastructure looms in deep space, silhouetted against a vast ringed gas giant at the horizon, with geometric running lights tracing through the void amidst a starfield of cold pinpricks against absolute black. The entire atmosphere is monumental, minimal, and intensely cold, defined strictly by titanium silvers, cyan-white radiance, and deep void black, completely devoid of warm tones or organic textures.
```

## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
Cold brushed titanium surface with precision-machined edges and micro-etched detail lines. Cryogenic blue-white edge illumination — cold internal glow emanating from letter seams, the photonic quality of light bending near a gravitational event horizon. Hard vacuum atmospheric quality: zero ambient scatter, maximum edge contrast, each hairline stroke reading as a laser-cut threshold between titanium and void. Surface texture: aerospace-grade matte titanium with subtle anisotropic grain direction. The visual language of modern hard science-fiction cinema — monumental, minimal, cold, inevitable. Forbidden: warm tones, organic textures, soft edges, lens flare colour.
```

**Scene**

```text
Deep space operational environment — a colossal space station or alien megastructure silhouetted against a ringed gas giant at the horizon, its running lights tracing cold blue-white geometry through the void. Hard vacuum starfield: maximum-contrast cold stars against absolute black. Letters catch a single cold directional source — the light of a distant star, entering frame from upper-left at a precise angle, casting razor-edge technical shadows. One anamorphic streak: cold cyan-white, horizontal, transient. The scale of the scene dwarfs everything. Interstellar silence. No warm tones anywhere.
```

## Tips

- Short technological acronyms, vessel designations, or concise single words rendered in uppercase suit this monolithic aesthetic best.
- To vary the composition, replace the ringed gas giant with an eclipse or a distant accretion disk while maintaining the strict cold blue and titanium palette.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
