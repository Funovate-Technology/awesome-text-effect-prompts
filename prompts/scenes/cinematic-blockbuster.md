## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
Ultra-polished chrome metallic surface with razor-sharp mirror finish. Intense overhead directional spotlight casting hard dramatic shadows. Deep cinematic dark sky background with storm clouds or battle-scarred atmosphere. Volumetric god-rays piercing through darkness behind letters. Dual anamorphic lens flares — warm gold and cold blue streaking across frame. Forbidden: soft lighting, pastel tones, flat backgrounds.
```

**Scene**

```text
Epic summer blockbuster title reveal moment — storm clouds parting above a ruined battlefield skyline, golden volumetric light shafts breaking through, ember sparks drifting past chrome letters. Anamorphic scope frame, IMAX scale.
```

## Tips

- Short, high-impact words or heroic single nouns work best to convey a massive, epic scale.
- You can alter the mood by changing the warm golden god-rays and ember sparks to cold lightning or driving rain.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
