## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
Action movie title typography. Precision-machined brushed chrome-steel letters — not polished mirror finish, but tactical weapon-grade metal with fine directional grain. Intense asymmetric side-lighting from one direction: warm orange-gold key light striking one face of each letter, cool blue-white rim light on the opposing edge, creating razor-sharp contrast. Anamorphic horizontal lens flare streaking across the composition. Subtle motion-blur trails on letter edges suggesting high-velocity impact. Background: deep black with faint volumetric haze and sparse high-speed metallic sparks — not ash or embers, but the sparks of metal-on-metal friction. Overall mood: Hollywood action blockbuster, the second before impact. No rust, no scorched earth, no molten cracks. Photorealistic cinematic rendering.
```

## Tips

- Short, punchy words in all capital letters maximize the aggressive, heavy-metal impact of this treatment.
- Swap the warm orange-gold and cool blue rim lighting for contrasting hues like neon cyan and crimson to evoke a different cinematic subgenre.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
