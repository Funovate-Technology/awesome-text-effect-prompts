COLOR PRIORITY: keep the font's own colors if specified; otherwise electric blue, hot pink, acid green or golden yellow neon.

**Scene**

```text
Mounted on a dark textured wall — exposed brick or matte black panel. Moody ambient light, a slight haze in the air catching the neon glow. Bar or studio atmosphere.
```

## Tips

- Short phrases, single words, or punchy slogans mimic realistic bar and studio neon signage best.
- Swap the electric blue and hot pink for acid green or golden yellow to instantly shift the mood.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
