**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
Intimate candlelit ceremony light field — primary illumination from clusters of tall ivory pillar candles positioned just outside the frame, casting warm amber-orange directional light at 30–45 degrees with the organic pulse of real open flame; this raking warm light catches all forward-facing surfaces while the surrounding environment recedes into rich warm shadow. Background: deep jewel tones — midnight navy, deep burgundy, warm obsidian — that absorb most available light and create maximum luminance contrast around any illuminated subject. Secondary fill: a diffuse rose-gold or warm champagne light from the opposite side at 20–30% of key intensity, preserving shadow depth without flattening it.

Background atmosphere: champagne glass rims refracting amber light into vertical streaks, rose petal edges catching warm highlights, crystal chandelier droplets resolving into circular bokeh halos, wax candle surfaces with subtle orange-amber surface glow — all at extreme shallow depth of field, contributing warmth and occasion as pure bokeh shapes without competing with the illuminated subject.

Color temperature consistently warm throughout at 2800–3200K. Deep jewel-tone backgrounds (midnight blue, burgundy, charcoal) provide maximum luminance contrast. Subtle warm vignette darkens frame edges. The atmosphere of a private ceremony: intimate, still, held against time.

When no specific material is defined by the font: warm candlelight from 40-degree upper-left, deep midnight blue or burgundy background with soft warm vignette, champagne glass and rose petal bokeh at low opacity in background.
```

## Tips

- Milestone years, couple names, or romantic celebratory words harmonize naturally with this warm ceremonial aesthetic.
- Adjust the background balance toward midnight blue or warm charcoal to subtly shift the mood between rich romance and quiet elegance.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
