**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
a warm hand-lettered Teachers' Day greeting card — a complete flat illustrated card design filling the entire frame, not a photograph of a card. GROUND (suggested default, not fixed): lean toward a warm, saturated color filling the frame edge to edge as the card's own ground — a solid sunny yellow, blush pink, sky blue or warm apricot wash. A paler neutral (oat cream, chalk white, pale sage) is acceptable when a scene calls for a quieter register, but is not the automatic default — an all-neutral ground with no other color reads as thin and unfinished. LEGIBILITY (mandatory): unless the chosen font's own treatment names a specific ink color (gold foil, rose gold, chrome, a named color), the lettering must render in a deep tone with strong, clearly readable contrast against whichever ground color is chosen — never a pale or washed-out ink close to the ground's own value. PALETTE: warm and generously saturated — sunny yellow, blush pink, sky blue, apple red, leaf green, warm apricot — used freely across the ground, any illustrated elements and the lettering itself, never confined to one small accent zone and never washed-out or muddy. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own named material or color (gold foil, rose-gold, chrome, engraved metal), that treatment takes absolute priority and owns the letters; this style governs only the ground, the palette and the legibility requirement above. FALLBACK MATERIAL: if the font carries no material of its own, render the letters in a warm hand-lettered ink or marker finish, in a deep tone with strong contrast against the ground, so the handmade card feel always reads clearly.
```

**Scene**

```text
The text is the largest, clearest mass in the frame, set in the open middle of the desk with generous empty space around it. The desk objects are arranged behind and to both sides, resting on the surface with soft contact shadows, never printed flat onto the background. Shallow depth of field — nearest objects sharp with visible surface texture, the far edge of the arrangement softening. Background: a pale oat-cream wall or paper backdrop catching the window light.
```

## Tips

- Short celebratory phrases, subject names, or teacher greetings work best to keep the hand-lettered marker finish bold and readable.
- Shift the primary surface wash between sunny yellow, warm apricot, or blush pink to easily change the mood of the palette.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
