**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
a preppy Christmas phone wallpaper, background, card, gift tag or invitation that a classic, put-together family or a preppy girl would actually set or send. GRAPHIC LANGUAGE: crisp graphic patterns - tartan plaid, gingham, candy and ticking stripes, argyle - used as borders, background bands, panels or a patchwork of squares, with neat velvet or grosgrain bows, a small wreath or monogram crest, and the words on a clean cream or white panel; wallpapers keep the top third calm for the clock. LIGHT: clean, bright, even light, like a well-lit stationery flat lay, crisp soft shadows under bows and tags. COLOUR: classic red, kelly or hunter green and navy with cream, and touches of pink; one metallic gold-foil accent used in one or two places. TEXTURE: smooth card stock, the woven look of plaid and gingham, velvet and grosgrain ribbon, crisp gold foil. TIERS: the classic plaid tier is red and green tartan, navy bows and gold; the pink preppy tier is pink, red and green stripes and gingham with playful bows, bright and young. LETTERFORM COLOR PRIORITY: if the selected font defines its own letterform colour or material, that takes absolute priority; red, green or navy colours the letters only when the font does not specify. ATMOSPHERE: the plaid and stripe patterns, the bows and the cream panel always appear around the words regardless of letterform colour. When no specific material is defined by the font: default lighting - the words sit on a cream panel framed by tartan under clean, bright, even light.
```

## Tips

- Short holiday greetings, family names, or classic single words like 'JOY' or 'CHEER' look best within the structured frame.
- Introduce soft pink accents to the ribbon or stripes to shift the mood toward a brighter, more playful preppy look.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
