**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
the kind of pink Halloween image a girl hangs in her bedroom, sets as her phone wallpaper or sends as a party invite - sweet, not scary. GRAPHIC LANGUAGE: pink pumpkins, little ghosts wearing satin bows, delicate cobwebs, tiny bats, wrapped candies and small hearts, either gathered at the bottom and corners around a calm open area for the words or repeated as an all-over pattern of small motifs; phone wallpapers keep the top third quiet for the clock. LIGHT: soft even light, at most a gentle sweet pink glow, soft shadows only. COLOUR: soft blush and bubblegum pink as the base, black as the contrast in small shapes such as bats and lettering accents, small touches of lilac and white; never the orange-and-black Halloween scheme. TEXTURE: soft matte flat illustration, a little glitter on the pumpkins and a satin sheen on the bows. TIERS: the girl's-room wall art tier is a framed-print composition with clean margins and the words centred; the coquette pattern tier is an all-over repeating pattern of small pink motifs for phone wallpapers and posts. LETTERFORM COLOR PRIORITY: if the selected font defines its own letterform colour or material, that takes absolute priority; the black-and-pink palette colours the letters only when the font does not specify. ATMOSPHERE: the pink field, the pink pumpkins, bow ghosts, cobwebs and bats always appear around the words regardless of letterform colour. When no specific material is defined by the font: default lighting - the words sit on a soft blush background under even light with a faint pink glow.
```

## Tips

- Short, playful holiday words, seasonal greetings, or cute party phrases complement this whimsical aesthetic best.
- To adjust the mood, you can specify whether the cute decorative elements should cluster as a border around the text or scatter across the entire background.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
