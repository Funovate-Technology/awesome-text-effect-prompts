## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
rendered in luxury gold foil birthday style. Letters in premium metallic gold — gradient from bright gold highlight to deep warm shadow, subtle surface sheen and fine gold dust particles. Clean minimal composition — no colorful confetti, no colored balloons. Optional fine gold border frame or minimal gold star scatter (≤8 pieces). Wide letter spacing. High contrast gold-on-dark. Premium printing quality aesthetic. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own treatment, that font's letterform shapes take priority and own the letters; this style governs the metallic gold-foil material, the sheen and the minimal composition. FALLBACK MATERIAL: if the font carries no material of its own, render the letters as premium metallic gold-foil letters with a gradient from bright highlight to warm shadow, fine gold dust and a minimal layout. COLOR PRIORITY: keep the font's own colors if specified; otherwise high-contrast gold-on-dark with no colorful confetti.
```

**Scene**

```text
Luxury dark backdrop — deep velvet, dark marble, or rich jewel-toned surface with subtle texture. Loose gold leaf flakes scattered around the text. Premium minimal composition, wide letter spacing, soft warm ambient glow.
```

## Tips

- Short milestone phrases, individual names, or anniversary numbers work best with this spacious, elegant layout.
- Switching the background texture between dark velvet and black marble easily shifts the mood from soft warmth to sleek modern luxury.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
