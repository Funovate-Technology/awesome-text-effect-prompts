# Dry Brush

> Raw dry brush and paint brush lettering with heavy texture, bristle spread, and imperfect grain. Bold paint-loaded strokes, grungy distressed edges, and authentic hand-painted energy. Perfect for streetwear, music branding, sports, packaging, and any project needing raw tactile power.

## Standalone prompt

Replace `{{TEXT}}` with your words. Written for general image models; tested on `gemini-2.5-flash-image`.

```text
Render the text "{{TEXT}}" exactly as written, character by character. The words are rendered in a bold dry brush paint style, as if created with a heavily loaded paintbrush. Thick impasto strokes define the letterforms, showcasing distinct bristle spread across their surfaces. Raw, gritty texture appears throughout, featuring visible individual bristle lines, organic imperfections, and distressed grain. The edges of each mark are rough and torn where the paint runs thin, while thick paint buildup gathers at the stroke centers. Rendered in a single bold, raw off-black paint, the lettering conveys raw, high-contrast energy. The text rests on a rough textured paper surface, creating an authentic, physical, hand-painted scene. The paint slightly glistens at the thick stroke centers while remaining completely dry and matte along the frayed outer edges, leaving {{TEXT}} as the only legible text in the image.
```

## Original FontVibe style layer

This is the style layer FontVibe itself uses. It is **not** a complete prompt: on FontVibe it is combined with a font description, a font reference image and the model routing for that style, so some sentences refer to "the chosen font". Use the standalone prompt above with other tools.

**Surface, colour & lighting**

```text
rendered in dry brush paint style. Bold letterforms created with a heavily loaded paint brush — thick impasto strokes with visible bristle spread across each letterform surface. Raw gritty texture throughout: individual bristle lines visible, rough torn edges where paint runs thin, paint buildup at stroke centers. Distressed grain and organic imperfection in every mark. High-contrast raw paint energy. LETTERFORM PRIORITY: when the chosen font carries its own treatment, that font's letterform shapes take priority and own the letters; this style governs the dry-brush paint medium, the bristle texture and the raw gritty grain. FALLBACK MATERIAL: if the font carries no material of its own, render the letters as thick impasto dry-brush strokes with visible bristle spread, rough torn thin edges and paint buildup at stroke centers. COLOR PRIORITY: keep the font's own colors if specified; otherwise a single bold raw off-black paint.
```

**Scene**

```text
On rough textured paper or aged wood surface. Paint slightly glistening at stroke centers, matte and dry at edges. Raw, physical, hand-painted atmosphere.
```

## Tips

- Short, punchy words or action verbs work best to complement the forceful, energetic strokes of the dry brush.
- Try changing the off-black paint to a bold primary color like crimson red or deep ultramarine to give the gritty brush marks a different visual impact.
- Short words (1–3 words, under ~15 letters) come out most reliably on general models; long phrases are where spelling breaks first.
- Results are best on FontVibe, where this style is paired with a real font, a font reference image and the model it was tuned on. General models get the material, colour and mood but choose their own letter shapes.
